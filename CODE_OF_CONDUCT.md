# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all members of the GitTogether community. We value the participation of every individual and want all contributors to have a positive, respectful experience.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

✅ **Be Respectful**
- Treat all community members with dignity and respect
- Listen actively to others' perspectives
- Value diverse opinions and backgrounds

✅ **Be Inclusive**
- Welcome and support newcomers
- Use inclusive language
- Make space for underrepresented groups

✅ **Be Constructive**
- Provide actionable feedback
- Focus on ideas, not individuals
- Help others grow and learn

✅ **Be Professional**
- Keep discussions on-topic
- Respond thoughtfully to criticism
- Assume good intentions

## Unacceptable Behavior

❌ **Harassment** — Any form of harassment based on protected characteristics (race, gender, identity, etc.)

❌ **Discrimination** — Discriminatory comments or exclusionary behavior

❌ **Intimidation** — Threats, stalking, or doxxing

❌ **Abuse** — Personal attacks, name-calling, or intentionally hurtful comments

❌ **Spam** — Irrelevant content, unsolicited promotion, or commercial spam

❌ **Unwanted Attention** — Unwelcome advances or persistent contact after request to stop

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

### Reporting Process

1. **Document** the incident (date, time, people involved, what happened)
2. **Report** to our Code of Conduct team: `conduct@gitogether.dev`
3. **Include**:
   - Description of what happened
   - When and where it occurred
   - Who was involved
   - Any relevant links or screenshots (if applicable)
   - What action you'd like taken (if any)

### Confidentiality

- Reports are kept confidential
- Reporters' identities protected unless they consent to disclosure
- Investigation details shared only with those who need to know

### Response

- Acknowledgment within 24 hours
- Preliminary investigation within 48 hours
- Resolution within 7 days (or explanation for delay)
- Appropriate corrective action taken

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may result in:

1. **Warning** — First-time minor violations
2. **Temporary Suspension** — Repeated violations or serious incidents
3. **Permanent Ban** — Egregious or repeated severe violations

## Appeal Process

If you disagree with an enforcement decision:

1. Email `appeals@gitogether.dev` within 14 days
2. Include new information or context
3. Decision made within 7 days
4. Final decision is binding

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues, PRs, and discussions
- Discord server (if applicable)
- Conferences and meetups organized by GitTogether
- Social media discussions about GitTogether
- Any official GitTogether communication channels

**Does NOT apply to:**
- Private conversations or third-party platforms
- External projects or communities
